<section class="sd-welcome">

  <p class="sd-lead">
    I study how environmental conditions shape the behaviour and
    physiology of lizards.
  </p>

  <p>
    My current research focuses on the Kashmir rock agama
    (<em>Laudakia tuberculata</em>) across environmental gradients
    in the Western Himalayas.
  </p>

  <p>
    More broadly, I am interested in how organisms respond to the
    physical environments they inhabit, and how those responses
    influence their persistence in changing ecosystems.
  </p>
