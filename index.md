Hi and thanks for visiting my website. 

I am Saumitra, a PhD student at FLAME University, Pune. I am a semi-professional dilly-dallier and also hold a Master’s degree in Ecology from Pondicherry University. While I have experience working on diverse taxa; my primary interest lies in the herpetofauna (kindly read this as obsessed with frogs, snakes and lizards). 

Prior to joining FLAME University, I have had to opportunity to explore different aspects of Indian herpetofauna such as their diversity, distribution and abundance across the subcontinent, thermal physiology and sleep ecology. 

Along with exploring stunning landscapes and looking for/at beautiful organisms, these experiences also eventually led me to a broader question: how do environmental constraints influence species persistence in changing ecosystems? To that extent, I am currently studying the behavioural and physiological responses of the Kashmir rock agama (*Laudakia tuberculata*) across environmental gradients in the Western Himalayas.
