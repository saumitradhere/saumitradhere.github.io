Hi, I'm Saumitra, a semi-professional dilly-dallier and also hold a Master’s degree in Ecology from Pondicherry University. While I have experience working on diverse taxa, my primary interest lies in herpetofauna (kindly read this as being obsessed with frogs, snakes and lizards).

Prior to joining FLAME University, I have had the opportunity to explore different aspects of Indian herpetofauna such as their diversity, distribution and abundance across the subcontinent, thermal physiology and sleep ecology.

Along with experiencing stunning landscapes and beautiful organisms, these experiences have also shaped my current research trajectory, eventually leading me to a broader question: how do environmental constraints influence species persistence in changing ecosystems? 

To that extent, I am currently studying the behavioural and physiological responses of the Kashmir rock agama (*Laudakia tuberculata*) across environmental gradients in the Western Himalayas.
